# Colvenna Catalog `v1.0.0-rc.4`: performance acceptance

## 1. Verdict

**`PASS`**, by [terms.md](terms.md) section 1: every judged metric of every scenario was measured with enough samples and met its target, with no error where none is allowed; P1c is observed and counts for nothing. Only `PASS` meets design 9.4, so the release check's performance item is **met**.

- What decided it: every judged row passed (section 5), in both forms; no row was left unjudged for want of samples: P1b's whole commit had its 1,200 samples in each form, and P5's credential misses are reported, not judged, by the terms (section 4).
- The judged runs: P1a `p26110104247df0` (EKS) PASS; P1a `p2611010447510d` (serverless) PASS; P1b `p2611010510b3ea` (EKS) PASS; P1b `p26110105391cce` (serverless) PASS; P1c `p2611010629684f` (EKS) OBSERVED; P1c `p261101064748ee` (serverless) OBSERVED; P2 `p26110107061a72` (EKS) PASS; P2 `p26110107480f7f` (serverless) PASS; P3 `p26110108393231` (EKS) PASS; P3 `p26110109020b2d` (serverless) PASS; P4a `p26110111049214` (serverless) PASS; P4b `p2611011427ce85` (serverless) PASS; P5 `p26110109261acd` (both forms) PASS. Runs that were stopped or refused: none; every run is judged once.
- What the verdict covers: the default sizes of terms.md section 2, the loads of section 4, in `us-west-2`. It is not a capacity limit (section 7).

What changed since `v1.0.0-rc.2`'s acceptance (its document gives the failures this candidate's fixes and the revised terms address):

- **P2, P5 on the EKS form: Nagle's algorithm is off** ([#37](https://github.com/colvenna/colvenna-catalog/issues/37), fixed by [#41](https://github.com/colvenna/colvenna-catalog/pull/41)): `getTable` p95 at 200/s is 7.7 ms here against `v1.0.0-rc.2`'s 64.8 ms; the client's request line and the whole operation now differ by about a millisecond.
- **P1a on the EKS form**: 12.532 commits a second, against `v1.0.0-rc.2`'s 8.703, with the same eight writers back to back.
- **P1b's load, revised on 2026-11-01** ([#83](https://github.com/colvenna/colvenna-catalog/issues/83)): 1 commit a second offered on the EKS form for 20 minutes and 0.5 a second on the serverless form for 40 minutes, within one table's commit capacity, its targets unchanged; every commit of both windows done, none failed. The earlier load, 2 commits a second, ran as P1c, observed: 1.925 and 1.78 commits a second done.
- **P5's credential misses, revised on 2026-11-01** ([#91](https://github.com/colvenna/colvenna-catalog/issues/91)): their latency is reported, not judged (384 on the EKS form, 638 on the serverless form, fewer than the 1,000 a judged percentile needs), and P3 judges it (1,120 and 1,090 misses, both within their targets).

## 4. Scenarios, method and targets

As [terms.md](terms.md) section 4 fixes them; the harness ([`tests/performance/README.md`](../../tests/performance/README.md)) runs each as one command, `perf.py run <scenario>`. In brief:

- **Client-observed latency** of a request is the Paimon client's own log line, per attempt; **operation latency** is the load tool's timing around one Paimon call, the client's retries included. Open-loop requests are timed from when each was due; a request is sent on time when it started within 100 ms of that.
- **Joins**: by the server's `X-Request-Id`, which the client's line carries; an operation's attempts are its thread's lines between its start and end. A credential request is a miss or a hit as its `ISSUE_DATA_TOKEN` audit event says; a commit refused on a stale base is a conflict, not an error.
- **Percentiles**: nearest rank over every sample of the window, warm-up excluded; 1,000 samples for a judged percentile (P4: 40).
- **Open-loop percentiles** are of the requests answered; those that failed are errors, counted apart. The client's request line ends at the answer's headers, so for P2 and P3 it is reported beside the judged latency, which includes reading the answer.
- **Choices the terms leave to the method**: P1b's whole commit is the `commit` call (conflict retries included); P3b's 20 tables are asked once each, then a minute of warm-up at the rate, before its window; P5's warm-up is 2 minutes; P5 reports apart the requests a serverless environment that started in the window served first.
- **P5's credential misses**: about 360 a form (10 % of 2 a second for 30 minutes), fewer than the 1,000 a judged percentile needs; by the requester's decision of 2026-11-01 ([#91](https://github.com/colvenna/colvenna-catalog/issues/91); terms.md, P5) their latency is reported, not judged, and P3 judges it. A miss that failed is still one of P5's errors.

No judged run differs from the terms' durations or loads: every `result.json` has `reduced: false` and `official: true`.

## 7. Limitations

As terms.md section 7, and of this measurement:

- One Region, the default sizes, synthetic load from inside the same VPC; clients in other VPCs or Regions are not measured.
- The databases are burstable `db.t4g.small`; their CPU and lowest credit balance are in each run's `summary.md`. No run spent its credits.
- P4's idle samples depend on Lambda's reaping, which the test does not control: they are observed only.
- The serverless form's `install.sh` was run one at a time in the Region for this acceptance (#94); a throttled control-plane call is not part of what is measured.
