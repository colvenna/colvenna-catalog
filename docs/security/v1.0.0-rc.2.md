## 3. ASH: the new findings and their handling

ASH found 10 actionable findings, detect-secrets' "Base64 High Entropy String", none of them at the previous record:

| File (at B) | Lines | What the value is |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `tests/deploy/test_environment.py` | 15, 16 | Made-up names of a test environment's CDK functions' log groups, as CloudFormation cuts them (`ColvennaCatalogTest…-LogRetentionaae0aa3c5b4d-AbCdEfGhIjKl`) |
| `tests/deploy/test_serverless.py` | 717, 718 | The same, of an install's `Storage` stack |
| `tests/deploy/test_entrypoints.py` | 173, 174, 189, 190, 191 | The same, of an install's `Storage` and `Eks` stacks |
| `tests/uat/test_dep.py` | 87 | The same, cut to 25 characters |

They came with the tests of [#11](https://github.com/colvenna/colvenna-catalog/issues/11) (the truncated log groups). Each was checked by hand: a function name with a made-up suffix and a public CDK construct hash, not a secret. They are in tests, which neither the image nor the chart carries.

**Handling: suppressed as verified false positives**, each marked on its line with `pragma: allowlist secret - <reason>` (section 3 of the scans page), in [#26](https://github.com/colvenna/colvenna-catalog/pull/26) (merged as [`5227e23`](https://github.com/colvenna/colvenna-catalog/commit/5227e23ec76b6138c0446ef9393fac737fd42ad0), after the tag, which keeps the candidate B plus `release/` only). Its two-axis review found that detect-secrets honours a mark only at the start of its comment, so two marks first written after earlier comment text were not seen; they were moved, and `scripts/security/test_ash_config.py` now checks every mark in the tree is where detect-secrets reads it. `scan.sh` on the change's head, `0041aae1e033ae05b99052bdd0f378d0ea2494cf`: ASH exit 0, 0 actionable, detect-secrets 59 suppressed (49 before, and the 10).

Also from ASH, as section 3 of the scans page counts them: 288 LOW bandit findings (`subprocess` in tests and scripts, test fakes, test asserts, an `xml.etree` import), checkov 5 suppressed, every other scanner 0; cdk-nag is `MISSING` in ASH as at the baseline (section 4: it runs in every synthesis instead).

## 4. The server's image and what Paimon's jars bundle

**Java, in the image.** Trivy's 11 HIGH and 8 MEDIUM and grype's 11 High and 8 Medium are exactly the advisories section 6.1 lists for `paimon-format-2.0.0.jar` (Jackson 2.14.3: CVE-2025-52999, CVE-2026-68494, CVE-2026-89425, CVE-2026-54512, CVE-2026-54513, CVE-2026-68497, CVE-2026-91776, CVE-2026-91777, CVE-2026-19032, CVE-2026-54514, CVE-2026-54515, CVE-2026-59889, CVE-2026-77310, CVE-2026-83557; protobuf-java 3.19.6: CVE-2024-7254; commons-lang 2.6: CVE-2025-48924) and `paimon-api-2.0.0.jar` (httpcore5 and httpcore5-h2 5.3.6: CVE-2026-54399, CVE-2026-54428; httpclient5 5.5.1: CVE-2026-64607).
